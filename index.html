Hello! My name is Jorge Centeno Cruz, and I am from Tegucigalpa, Honduras. I am 23 years old. I enjoy playing fútbol and video games, and I love watching movies and listening to music. I also know how to play the piano and the ukulele. One of my greatest joys is spending time with my family. In this picture, my family and I were visiting the San Fernando Fortress. The San Fernando Fortress (Fuerte de San Fernando de Omoa) is located in Omoa, on the northern coast of Honduras. It was built by the Spanish in the late 18th century to protect the region from pirates and foreign invasions. The fortress is one of the largest and best-preserved colonial fortifications in Central America, with thick stone walls, cannons, and a strategic view of the Caribbean Sea.
